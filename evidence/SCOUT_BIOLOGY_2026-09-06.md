# Phase 1 biology scout — initial source map

Access date for every source below: **2026-09-06**. This is a bounded set of diagnostic constraints, not a diagnosis, review verdict, or proposed repair. Paper results are literature observations, not measurements produced by this programme. No Phase 2 ideas were developed.

The programme's human/animal competence premise remains unverified at its stated breadth. The evidence below separates producing informative experience, using experience in a changed action situation, storing something, and expressing a retained response. None demonstrates their conjunction across open-ended worlds.

## B1. Producing informative actions does not establish that the resulting knowledge was acquired

**Evidence class:** human behavioural intervention.

**Primary source:** Schulz, L. E. & Bonawitz, E. B. (2007), *Serious fun: Preschoolers engage in more exploratory play when evidence is confounded*. Published journal article, not a preprint. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1037/0012-1649.43.4.1045); [author-hosted manuscript](https://eccl.scripts.mit.edu/papers/bonawitzandschulzseriousfun.pdf). Accessed 2026-09-06. Read manuscript Methods, Results, and General Discussion, PDF pages 4–9.

**Methods/result bounds:** Preschoolers experienced a two-lever toy with causes activated jointly or separately, then chose between it and a novel toy during free play. Separate controls matched effect exposure, trials, and opportunity for independent play. Confounded evidence increased exploration of the familiar toy. In that condition, 12/16 children independently manipulated both levers. The authors explicitly state: “Our experiment does not address this directly” about whether children learned causal relationships from their interventions. Participants were predominantly white and middle class; many were science-museum visitors.

**Constraint/interpretation:** Diagnostic action selection and learning from its outcome need separate evidence. This study supports sensitivity to evidential ambiguity, not effortless causal mastery, retention, or recombination.

**Unfair artificial comparison:** Comparing children's opportunity to manipulate visible, familiar affordances with an artificial learner restricted to passive confounded observations or unspecified actions. Counting informative play as correct knowledge for humans while requiring successful transfer from AI would also mismatch outcomes.

## B2. Knowing an action can be executed does not establish spontaneous transfer of an observed relation into that action

**Evidence class:** animal behavioural intervention and human behavioural experiment.

**Primary source:** Taylor, A. H. et al. (2014), *Of babies and birds: complex tool behaviours are not sufficient for the evolution of the ability to create a novel causal intervention*. Published journal article, not a preprint. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1098/rspb.2014.0837); [author-hosted full article](https://www.alisongopnik.com/Papers_Alison/Taylor%20et%20al%20Proceedings.pdf). Accessed 2026-09-06. Read Materials and Methods, Results, and opening Discussion, printed pages 2–5.

**Methods/result bounds:** Subjects accidentally knocked a block onto a platform, releasing a reward; test trials required lifting the block from outside into the apparatus. None of five intervention-group crows solved it, while three separately shaped crows could execute the required behaviour. Sixteen of 22 toddlers solved; one solved before observation. The paper separately reports substantial technical/experimenter exclusions, attrition, and a smaller video-analysis sample. Some successful children tried incorrect holes first.

**Constraint/interpretation:** Learning a rewarded action and generating a changed action from an observed relation are distinguishable demands. A negative transfer result does not localize failure uniquely to causal representation: attention, affordance interpretation and prior experience remain alternatives. This is not a species-wide incapacity demonstration.

**Unfair artificial comparison:** Treating identical apparatus as matched prior experience or perception. The children had language, caregiver presence and human artifact experience; the birds had different shaping histories. An AI comparison must expose equivalent action affordances and distinguish pre-existing skills from within-task learning.

**Interpretation remains disputed:** Taylor et al.'s [2015 invited reply](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4632611/) (accessed 2026-09-06; full text returned in search) acknowledges the human/animal prior-experience problem and disputes whether earlier successes establish novel causal interventions. This is an author argument, not additional experimental evidence. The opposing [Jacobs et al. comment](https://doi.org/10.1098/rspb.2014.2504) was located but its full text could not be reliably read in this pass. Do not resolve that dispute from the reply alone.

## B3. Failure under the normal retrieval cue need not mean the information has been erased

**Evidence class:** animal intervention, with animal neural recording and histology.

**Primary source:** Ryan, T. J., Roy, D. S., Pignatelli, M., Arons, A. & Tonegawa, S. (2015), *Engram cells retain memory under retrograde amnesia*. Published Science article, not a preprint; [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1126/science.aaa5542); [full author manuscript](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5583719/). Accessed 2026-09-06. Read full Main Text and figure legends. The separate methods supplement was not accessible; see request below.

**Methods/result bounds:** Learning-active mouse dentate-gyrus cells were tagged; anisomycin followed contextual fear conditioning. Natural contextual cues elicited reduced freezing, but direct optical activation of tagged cells elicited freezing. No-shock and disrupted-encoding controls constrained nonspecific fear explanations. Subsequent natural recall remained impaired. Physiological connectivity observations accompanied this intervention; they do not themselves establish the storage substrate.

**Constraint/interpretation:** The normal input-to-behaviour path and experimentally recoverable experience-dependent state are separable. The authors' stronger connectivity-storage account is a hypothesis. Protein-synthesis-inhibitor amnesia, artificial activation, and fear behaviour bound generalization; neither ordinary forgetting nor human conceptual knowledge was tested.

**Unfair artificial comparison:** Giving biology experimenter-selected access to learning-tagged cells while testing AI only with ordinary cues. Conversely, inferring erasure from either system's failed behavioural output alone is unsupported. Supplement-dependent technical reliability remains provisional.

## B4. Persisting output, persisting carriers, and replenishment of carriers are different claims

**Evidence class:** animal intervention and longitudinal recording/histology; biology beyond neuroscience.

**Primary source:** Hammarlund, E. et al. (2017), *Plasma cell survival in the absence of B cell memory*. Published Nature Communications article, not a preprint. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-017-01901-w); [full article](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-017-01901-w). Accessed 2026-09-06. Read Results, Discussion, and Methods including allocation, depletion, flow cytometry, histology, antibody assays and statistical analysis.

**Methods/result bounds:** Six vaccinated male rhesus macaques were followed longitudinally; four received B-cell depletion and removal of spleen/draining lymph nodes, with two controls. Antigen-specific serum antibody persisted; BrdU-labelled cells with plasma-cell morphology were detected at the decade endpoint. Allocation was nonrandomized. The small groups, assay detection limits, unexamined intestinal sites and morphology-based cell identification limit claims of complete precursor elimination or identical cell histories.

**Constraint/interpretation:** The short lifetime of a secreted product does not determine the lifetime of its production capability. Stable long-lived carriers can coexist with replacement of their outputs. This does not demonstrate knowledge surviving wholesale replacement of the carriers, and immune recognition is not causal world modelling or arbitrary recombination.

**Unfair artificial comparison:** Calling stable antibody output proof of continually relearned memory, or matching antibody molecules to learned parameters while ignoring surviving antibody-producing cells. Any comparison must identify which physical or computational state actually persists.

**Additional primary boundary check:** Langley, W. A. et al. (2022), *Persistence of Virus-Specific Antibody after Depletion of Memory B Cells*. Published journal article, not a preprint. [Full article and DOI](https://journals.asm.org/doi/10.1128/jvi.00026-22). Accessed 2026-09-06. Read relevant Results, Discussion, and Methods through memory-B-cell assays. Human-CD20 transgenic mice recovering from acute viral infections received repeated rituximab. Serum assays and bone-marrow ELISPOT showed an early decline followed by slower decline of antibody responses; splenic memory-B-cell assays checked depletion. The authors could not exclude spared tissue-resident memory B cells and did not establish why the early drop occurred. This supports a bounded durable-effector observation, not total independence from precursor replenishment in every tissue. The method measures actively secreting plasma cells, not all potential immune memory.

## What follows from these sources, and what does not

The useful diagnostic partition is an inference to investigate: an agent may fail before obtaining discriminating evidence; after obtaining it but before using it in a different action situation; or during access to surviving state. These are possible locations of failure, not ranked causes in current AI. The papers do not establish that any of these explains a particular artificial system.

Required further work includes stronger recombination evidence, development with prior experience controlled, natural forgetting, and primary cellular evidence about structure through molecular turnover. This pass does not satisfy the full breadth of Phase 1 biology or the archaeology requirement. No evidence presented here licenses the claim that humans and animals solve the full programme demand easily.
