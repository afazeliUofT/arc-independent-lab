# Phase 1: positive biological reuse, with acquisition and inference separated

**Access date for every URL: 2026-09-06.** Bounded research consultation sharing the PI's tools and filesystem; not independent review. This note supplements the persistence-focused biological notes. It proposes no engineering mechanism. The three main studies are published articles, not preprints. They come from an overlapping research collaboration, so they do not constitute three independent replications.

The positive result deserves equal weight with biological limitations: information acquired before its later appetitive significance is established can influence a response that was never directly rewarded. These studies also manipulate neural activity at different stages, helping distinguish acquisition from later use. Their experiments remain much narrower than autonomous discovery and lifelong transfer.

## BR1. Previously neutral relationships can support changed-outcome responding in rats

**Sharpe et al. (2017), Nature Neuroscience.** [Published article](https://doi.org/10.1038/nn.4538); [author-hosted corrected paper, methods and corrections](https://sharpelab.psych.ucla.edu/wp-content/uploads/sites/185/2018/09/Sharpe-et-al.-2017.-Nature-Neuroscience.pdf).

Initially unrewarded cue sequences preceded later cue–food conditioning. Dopamine-neuron activation at a cue transition overcame prior-association blocking; stimulation between trials did not. Suppression during another neutral transition reduced subsequent preconditioned responding while direct food conditioning proceeded. A separate, small devaluation contrast—five rats per condition—reduced responding to the never-food-paired cue after illness was paired with food. This supports outcome-sensitive reuse, beyond an unchanged scalar preference attached during initial exposure. It does not uniquely locate when all associative integration occurred.

The interventions were broad and did not reproduce a measured natural population pattern. Non-histological collection/analysis was unblinded. The corrected activation lasted **one second**, not two; appended corrections also concern figure keys, histograms and one plotted point. Main experimental Results, full Online Methods, relevant Discussion and all three appended corrections were read; supplementary-figure data and raw data were not audited. No deliberate memory reset or long interference series was tested.

## BR2. An early perturbation can cause a later reuse deficit without preventing direct conditioning

**Hart et al. (2020), eLife.** [Published article](https://doi.org/10.7554/eLife.59998); [primary full-text deposit including reviews](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/europepmc/webservices/rest/PMC7481003/fullTextXML).

Rats experienced A→B and C→D without food during those pairings, then B→food and D→nothing. Controls approached food more for A than C at an unrewarded probe. OFC inhibition only during initial cue pairings disrupted that contrast; the cue-by-group interaction was significant, not merely one significant comparison and one null comparison. Direct conditioning showed no significant group impairment. In a control-rat subset, food devaluation reduced initial responding to A.

All rats had previously received food-cup shaping. Thus, unrewarded cue pairings were embedded in a context with rewarded experience. Of 54 rats, 43 entered final analyses after stated exclusions. No matched inhibition-between-trials condition was included; attentional/contextual mediation remains possible. The paper does not prove that the manipulated population uniquely stores the relationship, or that a failed probe means erasure. Main text, complete Methods, figures' legends and published reviewer/author exchange were read; raw data were not reanalyzed.

## BR3. Humans combine learned associations, and post-learning perturbation can impair this use

**Wang et al. (2020), Journal of Neuroscience.** [Published article](https://doi.org/10.1523/JNEUROSCI.1680-20.2020); [primary full-text deposit](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/europepmc/webservices/rest/PMC7643295/fullTextXML).

Participants learned abstract-symbol pairs, then associations between second symbols and pleasant odors or odorless air. They were explicitly instructed to memorize pairs and later use them to infer outcomes for first symbols. After learning, stimulation at a lateral-prefrontal site selected by OFC connectivity reduced inferred reward predictions relative to sham; directly conditioned predictions showed no significant reduction. Forty-seven participants were analyzed after screening and exclusions.

Cue-pair recognition remained above chance; its group difference was nonsignificant, which does **not** establish equivalence or exclude memory mediation. The authors acknowledge that limitation. Stimulation was indirect, post-stimulation connectivity was not measured in this sample, and sham differed in sensory effects. All task learning and testing occurred in the main session; earlier visits established screening and targeting. Full main Methods, Results and Discussion were read. This is controlled, instructed relational reuse with a causal perturbation, not spontaneous discovery of an unknown action system.

## What the experiments require an explanation to account for

The following are our computational and methodological inferences, not additional biological measurements.

**Acquisition is relative to a future question.** An experience may be uninformative about today's reward while distinguishing relations needed after tomorrow's goal or outcome change. A learner that maps all such histories to exactly the same retained state cannot subsequently make history-specific decisions unless new evidence restores the distinction. This follows from the state inventory argument in `DIAGNOSIS.md` §1, independently of any neural implementation. It does not imply that every unrewarded event should be retained.

**Novel responding does not by itself identify online inference.** A response absent from training could arise from integration during later conditioning, integration between sessions, or computation at the probe. Perturbation timing helps locate necessary processing, but it is not a complete chronology of information flow. A post-learning deficit is particularly informative only if the component memories, stimulus processing, motivation and response demands are adequately controlled.

**A causal intervention on a component is not a unique causal explanation.** Removing activity can disrupt attention, encoding, maintenance, access or communication. Disrupting one measured response does not by itself distinguish these mediators. A strong account must explain the positive control responses and identify which alternatives its actual controls exclude.

**Retained state includes the continuing organism and its context.** These studies introduce new cue significance, and sometimes change outcome value; they do not remove all state carrying the earlier pairings. Ordinary gaps and additional conditioning are genuine continuity demands, but cannot be credited as survival through arbitrary overwriting, prolonged unrelated learning, or complete biological turnover. The experiments establish a short compositional dependency, not discovery of new primitives or transfer to a structurally novel world.

**Positive and negative evidence must meet the same attribution standard.** A nonsignificant control comparison is not proof of perfectly preserved function. Conversely, supplied cues, shaped behavior and lifetime priors narrow a successful result without making that result disappear. The fair conclusion is bounded competence under declared support, not either effortless biological generality or biological incompetence.

## Relation to the AI diagnosis and ways this account could be wrong

None of these studies directly compares its subjects with a current AI system. They therefore establish no empirical AI violation. They constrain explanations that claim to reproduce the corresponding behavior. An artificial system alleged to fail must receive specified comparable evidence and a declared surviving channel; a diagnosis must then locate whether it failed to acquire the component relation, retained insufficient information for the changed demand, or failed to use available components.

The narrow acquisition-before-utility claim would be undermined for an assay if the apparent transfer survived destruction of the relevant preconditioning contingency and was fully explained by stimulus generalization or uncontrolled direct reward. A stronger online-inference claim would be undermined if a cached representation acquired before the probe accounted for both the responses and perturbation effects. A claimed intact-memory/use dissociation would be weakened if an adequately sensitive component-memory measure mediated the impairment. These are refuters of interpretations, not claims that the published positive effects were absent.

Confidence is moderate that this family demonstrates short, outcome-sensitive relational reuse; lower that the current controls uniquely identify the full internal computation. Its relevance to broad AI failure is presently a task-level constraint, not evidence for a shared bottleneck. More neuroanatomical detail alone would not resolve that missing comparison.
