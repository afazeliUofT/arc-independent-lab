# Nearest-method flags after specification

2026-09-06. Written after raw generation, freezing, clustering and development of the operation specifications. These are one-line pointers to known methods, not a novelty audit, reduction argument or efficacy claim. No candidate was rejected using these flags.

| Operation | Nearest method or family currently known to the PI |
|---|---|
| C1 | Version-space learning and finite-state belief filtering; see [Mitchell, published IJCAI 1977](https://www.ijcai.org/Proceedings/77-1/Papers/048.pdf). |
| C2 | Bayesian experimental design and dual control; see [Klenske and Hennig, published JMLR 2016](https://www.jmlr.org/papers/volume17/15-162/15-162.pdf). |
| C3 | Regularized linear alignment to retained historical activations; the fitting operation points to [Hoerl and Kennard, published Technometrics 1970](https://doi.org/10.1080/00401706.1970.10488634). |
| C4 | Conjunctive concept learning and version-space scope restrictions; see [Mitchell, published IJCAI 1977](https://www.ijcai.org/Proceedings/77-1/Papers/048.pdf). |

All links accessed 2026-09-06. The IJCAI primary archive and publisher ridge-regression metadata were checked only to identify these flags. The complete Hoerl–Kennard methods were not read in this pass; no claim of an exact candidate reduction depends on its abstract. Klenske–Hennig's methods were already inspected during Phase 1; reopening its primary PDF here did not initiate a new audit. A full variable-by-variable comparison, distinguishing histories and any necessary methods retrieval belong to Phase 3 after human approval of `IDEAS.md`.

The repeated flag for C1/C4 is visible rather than hidden. Four specifications do not establish four novel mechanisms, and the presence of a familiar flag does not itself determine a reduction. Those are questions for the next phase, not conclusions of this one.
