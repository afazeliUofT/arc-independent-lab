# The focused biology pass: a nonempty generation input

PI synthesis, 2026-09-06, completed before candidate generation. This fulfills the first additional requirement in the published Phase 1 approval. The approved diagnosis remains unchanged.

The criticism in the approval is warranted: the earlier pass mostly disciplined our interpretation of memory experiments. That was useful diagnostic work, but did not sufficiently change the question from which a repair would be invented. This pass supplies two operational constraints.

**Earlier success can change the process that supplies the next evidence.** In the mouse antibody intervention, products of an existing response change subsequent competition and access to antigen. The engineering implication is conditional: a reduction in observed error or signal can reflect correction, self-produced masking, or a change in the world. These must not automatically be treated as the same learning event. This constraint is absent from a supplied, prediction-independent image stream; it is already present in some active-learning/control formulations, so it is not a claim about every artificial system. [Zhang et al., published JEM 2013](https://doi.org/10.1084/jem.20120150), accessed 2026-09-06. Primary-method account and named artificial contrast: `P2_BIOLOGY_IMMUNE_CONSTRAINTS_2026-09-06.md`.

**An acting learner cannot always take a discrepancy as a trustworthy instruction to change its action model.** In the finch study, larger imposed auditory mismatches elicited less compensation. The mechanism and optimality of that response remain unresolved. The computational demand, rather than an asserted avian algorithm, is to distinguish an action-effect change from an observation-channel change when observations arise through continued action. Our noiseless supervised witness removes that demand by construction. [Sober and Brainard, published PNAS 2012](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.1213622109), accessed 2026-09-06. Primary-method account and explicit identifiability deduction: `P2_BIOLOGY_ACTION_CONSTRAINTS_2026-09-06.md`.

These are constraints on the source and interpretation of learning evidence. They do not establish that biology learned arbitrary new causal variables, preserved communication/protection utility during the interventions, or selected an optimal experiment. Neither inspected intervention measures the full funded conjunction. In particular, ongoing song is not a measured guarantee of maintained communication, and immune binding competition is not an isolated manipulation of information alone. Those stronger claims are not generation premises.

The pass is therefore **nonempty but bounded**. It contributes to the first two existing diagnostic priorities rather than establishing a new universal cause. The design questions it licenses are: how can earlier competence censor later evidence; and what observation would distinguish a changed world from a changed observation relation? A generated idea must say what evidence and state it requires to address those questions. It need not be novel, feasible, biologically literal or correct at birth.

No mechanisms were generated in either source pass or this synthesis. Both source passes used shared tools and files and are consultations, not independent reviews. Unreachable alternative papers supply no adopted results and are not required dependencies. Their access failures are retained in the separate logs.
