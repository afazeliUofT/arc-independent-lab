## What the population reveals

The central divide is between choosing an answer in an available language and changing what a learner can ask or describe. The second population contains at least four separate acquisition moves: turning a failed equivalence into a new descriptive test; discovering the interchangeable parts of action procedures; acquiring ways to produce useful contrasts; and learning how a successful intervention changes access to evidence. These should not be reduced to one generic "grow the model" operation. A fifth move learns an access procedure for a changed memory carrier; that does not itself acquire a world relation.

There is also a substantial family in which the learner keeps multiple descriptions, translators or question interpretations. It matters because resolving a representation conflict by picking one language can discard useful alternatives. I would retain this family as a cross-cutting specification constraint in this round rather than make the entire family one candidate: its raw entries suggest several different operations, and a single plural-language architecture would otherwise obscure which acquisition changed.

The intentional extremes reveal two important endpoints. Exhaustive exposure postpones explanation until after collection; complete update autobiographies move recovery into a very large explicit memory channel. Neither endpoint may be dismissed for cost at this stage. They remain visible alternatives against which later claims about selective acquisition or retained-state recovery must be intelligible.

## Five development directions

These are recommendations for specification, not specified algorithms. Their selection prioritizes the unresolved acquisition problem and diversity of what changes. Their order keeps four acquisition moves ahead of the lower-priority access problem. No direction is selected because it is thought to be novel or computationally convenient.

### A. Convert an unresolved contrast into an executable descriptive primitive

Primary raw origins: R2-B1-01, R2-B1-02, R2-B1-03, R2-B1-16 and R2-B2-18. The learner stores a concrete pair of encounters that its current descriptive state merges although an observed continuation differs. It searches compositions of its own accessible history tests and action-observation procedures, performs candidate distinguishing procedures where permitted, and promotes an empirically supported test into the running description. Failed and unresolved obligations remain recorded.

The change is the addition of an operational predicate or history-state component, not merely a different posterior over a fixed set of complete world models. Its attraction is direct relevance to the first bottleneck's open extension: the learner must acquire the useful variable rather than receive it. The specification must separate a passive history test from a procedure that actively changes the world. A property defined by an invasive measurement is not automatically a pre-existing passive state variable.

Make the initial test grammar, observation tokenization, search order and promotion evidence explicit. A fixed grammar remains a supplied prior. An external assessor must not nominate the correct pair, label the latent factor, or certify a predicate with withheld future answers. The learner's failure detector should use permitted predictions and observations. Success would mean that an acquired test changes usable predictions or actions on subsequent encounters, beyond memorizing the original pair; a new symbol alone is insufficient.

### B. Learn reusable substitution roles from action procedures

Primary raw origins: R2-B1-04, R2-B1-05, R2-B1-10, R2-B1-12 and R2-B2-15. Start from acquired procedures with empirical effects. Exchange subprocedures, reorder them, or change permitted participants, recording where an effect survives and where it fails. Build provisional equivalence classes of substitutions and boundaries between pieces only from these outcomes. Later procedures compose roles discovered by that process.

This tackles recombination without supplying object roles, correct module boundaries or a catalogue of allowed substitutions. It differs from A: A makes a distinction expressible; B learns which acquired pieces can replace one another for a declared consequence. A single experiment can contribute to both, but the state updates and intended knowledge are different.

The specification must expose how candidate boundaries and exchanges are enumerated, what operational consequence is compared and which preparations were actually attempted. A participant identifier from the interface is not evidence that the learner discovered an object. Equivalence is conditional on tested contexts, and incomplete context coverage must not become a universal interchangeability claim. An acquired relation should support a new combination whose elements were encountered separately, with the source of its usable constraints traceable to actual substitutions.

### C. Learn experiment-building habits from the revisions they enable

Primary raw origins: R2-B2-01, R2-B2-10, R2-B2-11, R2-B2-23, with R2-B2-03 as access support. The learner mutates or composes its acquired procedures, stores their actual outcomes and later links them to changes in what its description learner can distinguish. Those links change the future generator of experiments, including reusable procedure fragments. Procedures that make further actions possible also contribute a concrete learned access relation.

The diagnostic attraction is obtaining evidence when a present model cannot express the relevant alternatives. It must not quietly become information gain computed from a supplied adequate model. Nor is selecting among a designer's fixed list of complete experiments sufficient to realize the raw proposal's strongest part: the specification must identify the update that creates an executable composition or mutation operator, and separate that update from merely assigning scores to existing experiments.

Delayed usefulness is not self-validating. If the description learner can invent arbitrary predicates, counting revisions alone rewards proliferation. Specify the observable downstream use that credits a revision, the credit assignment window and how unrelated events remain uncredited. The claim remains conditional on the permitted future-demand family and generator prior; it is not anticipation of arbitrary undisclosed future purposes.

### D. Learn procedures that restore evidence and separate measurement from intervention

Primary raw origins: R2-B1-06, R2-B1-07, R2-B1-08, R2-B2-05, R2-B2-06, R2-B2-13 and R2-B2-17. The learner repeats actions under varied accessible measurement arrangements and revisits measurements under changed action conditions, including the age or strength of its own products. It retains empirical relations between intervention conditions and what contrasts become observable. Restorative procedures become part of the acquired account of evidence access.

This is a distinct acquisition move: a successful policy can change the observation process, so more fitting to its resulting observations need not recover the missing contrast. The proposal respects the biological prompt as a constraint without claiming that any one biology result supplies this algorithm or establishes its efficacy.

Require actual selectable sensing or restoration operations in the interface; an oracle intervention that sets a hidden nuisance variable would supply the desired decomposition. Crossed observations do not identify a unique world/measurement attribution without additional assumptions, so competing explanations and untested cells must survive. Changed observation variability is evidence about access to a signal, not automatically evidence that a useful hidden factor has been discovered. An informative result would connect a learner-discovered restoration procedure to a subsequent usable distinction absent under the original successful policy.

### E. Learn a reader-construction process through observed carrier changes

Primary raw origins: R2-B3-01, R2-B3-06, R2-B3-07, R2-B3-09 and R2-B3-13; R2-B3-03 supplies the explicit retained-constraint variant. The learner makes permitted temporary changes to copies of its own state while consequences are still inspectable, searches reading programs that satisfy its own declared checks, and stores reusable repair fragments or search biases. A later changed carrier invokes that learned construction process using precisely the information allowed to survive.

The target is discovering access, not having an analyst provide the correct inverse, feature geometry or old query answers. The starting reading language and trial-change distribution are supplied biases and must be named. Temporary copies, current probes, stored responses, relational constraints, correction examples, repair programs and change histories are all channels or computation. A compact learned search bias can still contain historical information.

Keep retained-only recovery distinct from recovery that reopens world interaction. The latter may be useful, but success cannot establish that the answer was decoded from the changed carrier. A particularly discriminating later comparison would ask what the history-bearing carrier adds once the retained checks and learned search procedure are held fixed; a decoder that obtains the answer entirely from its check record has a different mechanism. Transfer beyond the artificial change distribution is an open empirical question, not an automatic consequence of successful simulated repairs.

## Constraints on the PI's specifications

Each developed operation should expose an initial bias and an actual learned object. No learner begins without a representational substrate; the claim is that the relevant predicate, role, procedure or reader is constructed within that substrate from permitted encounters. "The grammar can express it" is neither proof of acquisition nor proof that acquisition is vacuous. The diagnostic question is where the missing structure entered and which observed updates made it usable.

Description growth, experiment growth and reader growth require separate update rules. An integrated loop may later couple them, but its direction of dependence must be clear: who reports an unresolved contrast, who proposes a procedure, who observes its outcome, who decides what description changed, and who receives credit. Otherwise the specification can import the answer through an unspecified helper.

Reset semantics must inventory every retained object, including raw histories, unresolved pairs, procedure libraries, translations, counters, semantic checks and learned search weights. A declaration that "memory was cleared" is false if those objects still carry the relevant history. If two permitted histories leave all allowed state and subsequent observations indistinguishable while requiring different actions, the appropriate output is unresolved rather than an invented discrimination.

Nonselected raw entries remain visible. None is retired by this consultation. Clustering is reversible scientific organization after generation, not a disguised novelty or feasibility judgment. The PI may merge, split or choose different directions provided the final deliverable preserves the full population and explains the diversity of the selected underlying moves.
