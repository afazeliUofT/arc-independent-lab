## Drescher: predictive maintenance was already specified

The supplied 1989 dissertation explicitly describes host-trial evidence, reliable context evidence, predictions from other schemas and temporal persistence. Predicted positive and negative states can update a synthetic item; newer evidence overrides persistence, direct host trials take precedence over conflict, and uncertainty has an explicit state. **A duration-only description of Drescher's specification is therefore incorrect.** The earlier archaeology note had flagged this discrepancy rather than settled it; the supplied thesis now confirms the original predictive-maintenance provision. [Drescher dissertation, §3.4.3, printed/PDF pp.125–127](https://dspace.mit.edu/handle/1721.1/77702), accessed 2026-09-06.

The implementation evidence is narrower than the design. The thesis reports a pilot in an engineered microworld, curtailed by memory exhaustion; some illustrated coordination was only almost acquired, and its advanced representations were not yet used for substantial practical tasks. Those limits prevent treating the conceptual description as a demonstrated general solution. [Drescher dissertation, printed/PDF pp.128–129, 143–151, 204–210](https://dspace.mit.edu/handle/1721.1/77702), accessed 2026-09-06.

Still open: the 1991 book's correspondence with this dissertation, source-code fidelity, and the exact update timing and maintenance branches of the later comparator. The 2004 paper's duration-based attribution concerns its tested implementation; its contrast does not establish that the earlier design lacked prediction. [Holmes and Isbell, PDF pp.3–6 and 8](https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper/2004/file/18bb68e2b38e4a8ce7cf4f6b2625768c-Paper.pdf), accessed 2026-09-06. These unresolved issues do not negate the specification correction or show that the later numerical results were wrong.

**Follow-up trigger:** retrieve the relevant book passages or comparator code before making claims about exact implementation equivalence. No such equivalence is needed for the corrected conclusion above.

## Minton: utility validation measured less than the name can imply

The complete journal article confirms that retained control knowledge can reduce search while increasing the expense of each search step. It also reports useful learned knowledge, so it cannot support a blanket failure claim. Its learner receives a complete, consistent explanatory theory; unknown-law discovery is outside the demonstrated scope. [Minton, printed pp.364–366 and 377–386 / PDF pp.2–4 and 15–24](https://doi.org/10.1016/0004-3702(90)90059-9), accessed 2026-09-06.

**Qualification to the initial account:** subsequent utility validation measures matching cost and application frequency, but retains the search-savings estimate from the producing example. It is not repeated counterfactual measurement of actual savings. The validation ablation can also change the subsequent learning trajectory, so it does not isolate matching overhead with a fixed rule set. [Minton, printed pp.375–376 and 387 / PDF pp.13–14 and 25](https://doi.org/10.1016/0004-3702(90)90059-9), accessed 2026-09-06.

Still open: exact generator and training definitions, and full learning-plus-use costs. The expanded paper still delegates generator details to the earlier book/report; its evaluation excludes learning expense. The current evidence supports an application-cost distinction, not a quantitative lifetime break-even claim or reproduction of the original sampling. [Minton, printed pp.376–377 and reference 23 on p.390 / PDF pp.14–15 and 28](https://doi.org/10.1016/0004-3702(90)90059-9), accessed 2026-09-06.

**Follow-up trigger:** retrieve *Learning Search Control Knowledge: An Explanation-Based Approach* / CMU-CS-88-133 if original sampling or lifetime costs become necessary. Do not request the already supplied journal article again.

## Ryan: recoverable information is supported; clean storage/access isolation is not

The supplied main article and recovered supplement support a bounded dissociation: artificial stimulation can elicit experience-dependent behavior when natural contextual cues elicit less. This does not establish intact episodic content, a unique storage substrate, or complete restoration. [Ryan et al., main article](https://doi.org/10.1126/science.aaa5542), accessed 2026-09-06.

**Narrow the provisional control interpretation:** the encoding-disruption control reduces but does not abolish the light-associated response in the recovered supplementary data. A categorical account in which artificial access rescues retrieval failure but never encoding failure is too strong. Nonsignificant group differences are also not equivalence tests. A mixed explanation involving degraded information and changed access requirements remains possible. [Recovered supplement, S12 and methods](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/biostudies/files/S-EPMC5583719/NIHMS900165-supplement-Supplemental_Information.docx), accessed 2026-09-06.

Still open: publisher/deposit concordance, a version-dependent reconsolidation schedule, clustering in some cellular analyses, and how much information survives. Figure numbering differs between the published main article and the manuscript/deposit; their locators must not be silently exchanged. The method note records these mappings. [BioStudies deposit](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/biostudies/studies/S-EPMC5583719), [author manuscript](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5583719/), accessed 2026-09-06.

## Consequence for Phase 1

These sources strengthen the need to distinguish construction of a useful representation, maintenance of inferred current state, retention of acquired knowledge, access to that knowledge and the cost of applying it. They also demonstrate that useful learning and recoverable information exist within bounded settings. None determines the dominant cause of current AI failures, proves that scaling cannot help, establishes why a research line declined, or authorizes a repair. The diagnostic work must now test those distinctions rather than inherit a broad failure story from the brief.
