## The045 work and its boundary

The new bounded diagnostic adapter work addresses these source dependencies before a consolidated future profile:

1. Fit a prescribed frozen partition to exactly the supplied T, rebuild counts and preserve its source identity, with no feature selection, extra observations or assimilation of queued data.
2. Apply the common five-action chooser to fabricated ordinary histories and goal tokens, preserving rational comparison, lowest opaque-action ties and nonnumeric status.
3. Replay N1 from the acquired event sequence at its original RESET, immediate-trial and delayed-validation update boundaries. Preserve distinctions among issued, completed, assimilated and pending records; a final-table refit is not replay.
4. Reconstruct P00, P10 and P11 at the original pre/post-trial boundaries on the same validation histories. Check the exact count-plus-partition Brier decomposition. Raw pre/post improvement and gated operative credit are different quantities: count fitting can improve a forecast in a trial assigned zero credit because it selected no new predicate.
5. Delete each selected predicate with T and the remaining Phi fixed, alongside the prescribed ORDER0/1/2 comparisons. Any reuse between empty Phi and ORDER0 requires verified identity and explicit accounting.
