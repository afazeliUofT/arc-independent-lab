## Three leading causal questions in plain language

1. **What did learning select?** A rule can fit the experienced situations while depending on a coincidence that fails when the arrangement changes. The unresolved attribution is whether that selection actually mediates the relevant failure, rather than inadequate evidence or a later use problem.
2. **What evidence did the agent obtain?** Actions that do well now can leave a distinction unknown that matters later, even when an affordable experiment could have resolved it. This dependency is precise in supplied control models; its importance in strong general agents remains uncertain.
3. **Can the agent use what remains?** Later updates can damage the ordinary answer route while useful information survives. Our instrumented construction proves this local possibility under a supplied learner contract. It does not establish that general agents discover the recovery operation themselves or that hidden recoverability is widespread.

These are investigative priorities, not measured prevalence rankings. They put acquisition before assuming that already-correct knowledge merely needs protection. Positive AI adaptation, scaling and composition results prevent using the initial blanket failure claim as a finding. See [approved diagnosis](https://github.com/afazeliUofT/arc-independent-lab/blob/069bd7dc3db1cec6e9fc6ff67046547488f05353/DIAGNOSIS.md), repository copy accessed 2026-09-07.

## How comprehensive was the literature work?

It was broad and multidisciplinary, with primary-method inspection where claims depend on methods; it was purposive, not systematic or exhaustive. The historical pass includes schema learning, explanation-based learning, analogy, classifier systems, version spaces, adaptive resonance, production chunks, qualitative physics and dual control.
