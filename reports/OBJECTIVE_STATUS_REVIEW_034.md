## What the negative result taught

The earlier developed operations made important dependencies explicit, but they mostly manipulated distinctions after receiving much of their description language. Our audit did not turn that limitation into a novel acquisition mechanism. Returning to generation is therefore scientifically preferable to constructing an efficacy programme around a novelty claim we cannot support. The unselected first-pass ideas remain available; repeated or related ideas are allowed during generation.

None of today's entries has demonstrated that it can acquire the needed distinctions, maintain usable access or transfer to unfamiliar compositions. Specification may reveal that an attractive description still hides the answer in its interface, generator, stored responses or validation signal. That is work to do after the whole raw population is preserved; it was not used to prune the population while generating it.
