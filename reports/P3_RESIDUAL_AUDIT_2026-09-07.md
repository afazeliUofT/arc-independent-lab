## Corrections that change the interpretation

**My earlier wording about C4 discarding an alternative was too strong if read as information erasure.** C4 retains its seed, witnesses and test definitions. In the two-scope counterexample, those bytes still determine both A and B as admissible tied scopes. The actual response uses selected A and fails to consult the competing interpretation. This is premature commitment in the answer computation, not demonstrated loss from persistent state. Reconstructing alternatives has an explicit search and storage cost; it is not free. The new [joint boundary note](../evidence/P3_JOINT_AUDIT_BOUNDARY_2026-09-07.md) gives the proof and corrects the earlier interpretation without changing the old artifact.

**A matched reference implementation is not a historical priority certificate for a component copied into it.** T1-ref can match a conventional controller given the same belief and auxiliary C4 computation. That construction alone cannot prove that the disputed C4 wrapper appeared in a prior method. The new wrapper audit instead maps its specific predicates to independently described methods, while leaving whole-package historical identity unestablished. This explicitly narrows the inference from the earlier reference-completion argument.

**A programmed abstention is not automatically an impossibility proof.** For unrestricted linear histories, null-space ambiguity supplies the relevant proof. A finite history codebook can remove that ambiguity, even for collinear features. The frozen observer's collinear abstention must remain a statement about that reader unless two colliding histories are shown to belong to the same allowed family and interface.

## What the historical pass changed

The useful older work was concrete: Lovell's residualized regression, rough-set indiscernibility, local discernibility rules and knowledge-based planning. These are not merely biological or historical analogies. Their operations can be placed beside ours and compared directly. [Lovell, 1963 working paper/preprint](https://cowles.yale.edu/sites/default/files/2022-08/d0151.pdf), [Pawlak, 1982 published paper](https://wiki.eecs.yorku.ca/course_archive/2013-14/F/4403/_media/rough_sets.pdf), [Pawlak and Skowron, 2007 published method exposition](https://bcpw.bg.pw.edu.pl/Content/1970), [Petrick and Bacchus, 2002 proceedings](https://cdn.aaai.org/AIPS/2002/AIPS02-022.pdf), all accessed 2026-09-07.

I found no basis for saying these methods were universally abandoned. Lovell discusses technology-dependent calculation costs, and the later implemented estimability method shows continued use. More compute can increase feasible decomposition or scope-search sizes. It does not by itself validate a missing history contract, distinguish identical evidence records, or make the specified answer path consult alternatives. No further inaccessible book is needed to establish these bounded comparisons. [Lenth, 2015 published software method](https://journal.r-project.org/articles/RJ-2015-016/RJ-2015-016.pdf), accessed 2026-09-07.

The additional integrated antecedent also matters: known/unknown reports directing actual exploration were implemented in KWIK-Rmax, under declared realizability and learnability assumptions. Its optimistic planning differs from C2's Bayes-value rule, and it does not implement C4's complete diagnostics. This is evidence against novelty of the broad commitment, not a false exact reduction of the whole proposed system. [Li et al., 2011, §7.2 Algorithm 3](https://thomasjwalsh.net/pub/Li11Knows.pdf), accessed 2026-09-07.

The revised joint analysis separates real integrated historical antecedents from matched reimplementation. 2. **Recovery may hide privileged history or silently change its admissible family.** The observer mapping uses coordinates of current weights as regression responses; it does not train on old labels. The retained-basis reader and C3 cache remain separate information allowances. The identifiability proof states its unrestricted product domain, and its finite-codebook example demonstrates why a broader impossibility claim would fail.

The observer is stable only under conditions not broadly tested here. A nonempty unanimous supplied model class cannot certify that the true world belongs to it. No result here establishes prevalence across modern AI or automatically promotes a bottleneck rank.
