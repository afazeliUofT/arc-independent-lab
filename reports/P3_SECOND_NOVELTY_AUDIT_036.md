## What the audit establishes

The proposed repairs encounter a recurring problem: **an internally improved description or response need not identify newly usable world knowledge**. This is now a set of explicit deductions from the candidates, not an analogy. A partition can reduce pair conflicts without changing final outcome distributions; a finite substitution class can survive a counterexample outside its fixed context panel; a credit rule can ignore a real predictive improvement or reward a change for the wrong reason; a restored contrast can be a new contrast; and an old-response reader can learn from history without demonstrating access to a target relation.

Those limits concern our proposed mechanisms. They do not establish that current AI as a whole has the same defects, vindicate the funder's broad framing, or supersede the ranked diagnosis. No candidate was implemented or evaluated in this checkpoint. ## The strongest deductions and their limits

**N1: count coverage is not predictive information.** At a fixed grammar/table version, let `E` be the same-current-observation/action pairs with different outcomes, and `C_p` the pairs separated by a generated predicate. With the retained selected prefix `Phi`, the next gain is exactly `|C_p minus union(C_phi for phi in Phi)|`. This reproduces the whole selection step, including old choices; it does not mean fresh batch greedy on the final sample returns the online prefix.

The N1 audit gives four ordered episodes with a single varying lag-one observation. Each final partition cell has one outcome of each kind. Selection occurs after episode three, when its gain is positive; after episode four, the split and unsplit empirical and Laplace distributions coincide. The final unsplit cross-outcome pair count is four and the partition leaves two. This is not a claim of no transient or future benefit. It proves the narrower and relevant point that a positive pair-construction record cannot certify predictive improvement. This history uses the N1-alone input contract; it is not represented as an executed N3 resettable trace. Source: `evidence/P3_SECOND_N1_PRIMARY_AUDIT_036.md`, §§3–4, pinned in the manifest.

**N2: a counterexample can change an answer without changing its role.** The audit constructs a deterministic world whose only endpoint-one word is `ab`. With the empty context as the full one-context panel, acquired `a,b,a,b,aa,aa` trials make `a` and `b` equivalent on the panel. N2 conjectures that replacing the second `a` in `aa` by `b` preserves endpoint zero; it does not. Once `ab` is measured the direct answer is corrected, but the role still groups `a,b`. The missing information was not present in the earlier history, and the exact learner does not expand its already full panel. This witnesses a limit of the proposed reuse rule, not dishonesty in its `TRANSFER_CONJECTURE` status.

## Audit of the common acquisition-and-recombination claim

The broad through-line is that interaction should construct useful descriptions and procedures, preserve them, and use them in later encounters. It has integrated antecedents, including schema construction and curiosity/library learning. Novelty cannot be defended simply by saying that representation and action affect one another. The audited primary methods above establish real prior operations; they do not establish that a particular lineage has already achieved the full funded objective.

There are three different objects to keep separate:

1. **The broad research principle.** It is not new merely because it is expressed in acquisition/retention/recombination language. Historical architectures already connect learning and action, while differing in their supplied structure and what they actually demonstrate.
2. **The frozen executable subset.** N3 explicitly invokes N1. Their feedback loop is the specified combined operation: trials alter evidence; N1 may add a predicate; subsequent validation determines N3 utility; utility affects parent selection. The count/representation confound affects that loop directly. The N1-alone witness is not falsely promoted into a reachable whole-N3 result.
3. **A hypothetical joint N1–N5 controller and knowledge guarantee.** N2 and N4 have separate schedulers and query roles; N5 has a different state/query interface. Their allocation, arbitration and information handoffs have not been jointly specified. A proof about such a controller would concern a newly invented object. No whole-controller novelty kill, clearance or guarantee is issued for an unspecified system.

The persistent stores in the frozen contracts are real information channels. Surviving a volatile reset by retaining those stores is meaningful implementation behavior, but it does not demonstrate survival after those stores are erased. Physical restart, episode boundaries, external change and internal interference must remain different interventions. Likewise, composition in a supplied word/expression grammar is not by itself demonstrated transfer to unseen relations. The input interface and all source-history information remain part of the resource ledger.

The funder's earlier through-line requirement also remains active: **retain unresolved distinctions and distinguish unknown from an acquired answer**. Here that proposition has a testable, narrower form: every emitted status must be justified by the retained evidence and admitted computation, without treating untested cases or interrupted searches as established equivalence. N1 retains residual conflicts; N2 marks transfer conjectures; N4 distinguishes variable and untried effects; N5 marks source-trained conjectures. N3's positive utility is a selection signal, not a certainty certificate. Observation tables, context witnesses and partial-teacher methods already make evidence distinctions; mere status labels cannot establish a new integrated method. The N2 paired-world history proves that identical acquired evidence can leave a substitution undecided. Its honest conjecture label preserves that limit, but does not make the reuse correct. Thus uncertainty reporting is specified in parts; a joint policy that maintains and resolves the right uncertainties for future knowledge use is not established. This neither extends the first audit's whole-operation disposition nor silently drops the shared scientific claim.

The scientific judgement is therefore to avoid committing GPU time to a renamed combination at this point. This is because its evidential target and comparison are not yet strong enough, not because Narval is awkward to use. If a candidate survives the focused assessment, the later programme should size its actual computational needs and use HPC where justified; no laptop-sized treatment has been selected here.
