Three kinds of claim must remain distinct: mathematical equality of permitted behavior; membership in a historically established method family; and evidence that a particular historical implementation had the entire present contract. The first two do not automatically prove the third.

For C2, the task timing is substantive. The approved objective takes a weighted sum of task-specific minimum losses. If one decision must be chosen while the task remains hidden, the objective generally becomes a minimum of the weighted loss instead; those are different problems. The hard branchwise interaction allowance and model-inconsistent response to a zero-probability actual transcript must remain explicit. The typed guard is part of the candidate contract, not evidence that every historical POMDP implementation emitted that guard. [C2 audit][c2] and [review][verdict], accessed 2026-09-10.

For C3, with current anchor activations Z, old activations R, fixed reader W and positive lambda, define Y = RW and D = TW. The reduction is

\[
D=(Z^\top Z+\lambda I)^{-1}(Z^\top Y+\lambda W)
=\arg\min_D\{\lVert ZD-Y\rVert_F^2+\lambda\lVert D-W\rVert_F^2\}.
\]

Thus the old-query vector is the same at every permitted query, refresh and declared reset, not merely on the anchors. This is a behavioral identity under matched inputs and arithmetic, not a guarantee of successful recovery from collapsed current features. A held-out generalization benefit is possible, but it would not by itself distinguish C3 from its matched cached-response refit. Storage costs can differ and must be counted, rather than suppressed by describing both caches as “unlabeled.” [C3 audit][c3] and [review][verdict], accessed 2026-09-10.
