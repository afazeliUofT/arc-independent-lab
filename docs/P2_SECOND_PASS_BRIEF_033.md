## Why return to generation

The first developed operations preserved and manipulated useful distinctions after receiving much of the language in which those distinctions could be expressed. C1 receives its model family. C2 receives candidate experiment models, losses and priors. C4 receives its atoms and routing semantics. The observer receives a callable feature interface and training-history contract. Those are legitimate computational problems, but the audit does not show that these operations acquire the supplied structure. Making a closed set of supplied alternatives more careful does not, by itself, discover an alternative absent from that set.

The resulting question is a refinement of the approved acquisition bottlenecks: **how can interaction create or revise the descriptions in which later evidence and decisions become expressible?** This is a task for invention. It is not yet a demonstrated explanation for a frequency of failures in modern systems, nor a claim that earlier research ignored it. ## Generative inputs and order

Start from the approved diagnosis, raw first population and its clustering, the archaeology's actual abandonment constraints, and the biology generation records. Read the latter as computational prompts with their original evidence limits. Do not conduct a novelty search during generation or discard an idea because an antecedent is familiar. Recording this negative audit before reopening generation separates the activities; it does not require forgetting what was learned.

Use three sequential targets, keeping acquisition first:

1. **B1: description formation and revision.** How could the learner obtain distinctions, relations or alternative explanatory structures from its own encounters, instead of only choosing among an evaluator-completed family? What remains unresolved when different structures fit the same history?
2. **B2: obtaining evidence before knowing what matters.** How could interaction expose a useful distinction before the learner has an adequate model to predict which experiment separates it? Include feedback hidden or changed by the learner's own successful behavior, and uncertainty about the observation channel.
3. **B3: discovering and maintaining a usable reading operation.** When information survives in a changed state, how could the agent discover when and how to read it using its declared interface? Keep this separate from supplying the decoder, its feature geometry or the old answers. It remains lower priority than acquisition.

For each target, generate at least twenty-four rough entries before convergence: what changes, which bottleneck it attacks, and why it might help. This is a planned population size, not a measurement. Include historical prompts, biological constraints, inversions, combinations, orthodoxy violations and deliberately bad extremes. Preserve every entry. Mark origin tags without converting them into claims of efficacy or novelty.

Only after all raw batches are saved should the PI cluster them, choose three to five diverse moves and specify operations. Selection at that point is for diagnostic relevance and diversity, not a primary-method novelty audit. Add nearest-method flags only after specification. Write a new versioned second-pass deliverable; do not silently replace the approved first `IDEAS.md`.

## What a specification must make visible

A learner cannot infer arbitrary hidden worlds without priors or informative observations. “Acquire the representation” must not hide a complete solution in a generator, evaluator, feature callback or task description. During specification, expose the permitted observation/action interface; supplied grammar and learning biases; state that accumulates; operation that changes the description; and behavior when histories remain indistinguishable. These are accounting questions, not filters against rough ideas during generation.

Likewise expose information kept across resets, old examples or activations, decoder hints, trial computations and unsuccessful searches. Exact record retrieval, semantic abstraction, a decision policy and factual knowledge are different outputs. A correct relation requires an explicit domain and observation assumptions. Scope selection must not be described as erasing alternatives still retained in state.

No benchmark, GPU allocation or treatment is selected by this brief. The cluster remains available when a specified scientific comparison and measured workload justify it. Large compute cannot substitute for a missing operation. The next independent review will be a later finite task with its own concrete scope; run 032 is complete and will not be repeated.
