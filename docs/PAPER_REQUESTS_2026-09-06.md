1. **Gary L. Drescher, 1991, _Made-Up Minds: A Constructivist Approach to Artificial Intelligence_; the 1989 MIT dissertation is an acceptable first source.**
   - DOI or stable URL: https://dspace.mit.edu/handle/1721.1/77702
   - Why I need it: The 1987 description and a 2004 evaluation disagree about maintenance of inferred hidden state; the mature original specification must decide whether this is a real failure or an implementation difference.
   - What depends on it: The historical diagnosis of acquisition/state-inference limitations versus computational growth.
   - What I need from it: Whole book or dissertation, especially synthetic items, state maintenance, implementation, experiments and limitations.
   - If unavailable: Preserve the discrepancy and exclude a definitive claim about the mature original system; this materially narrows the archaeology finding.

2. **Tomás J. Ryan, Dheeraj S. Roy, Michele Pignatelli, Autumn Arons and Susumu Tonegawa, 2015, _Engram cells retain memory under retrograde amnesia_.**
   - DOI or stable URL: https://doi.org/10.1126/science.aaa5542
   - Why I need it: The accessible main article reports recoverable fear behaviour despite impaired ordinary recall, but the separate technical protocols and controls need inspection.
   - What depends on it: Whether this experiment substantiates the distinction between failed access and erased knowledge beyond the reported behavioural effect.
   - What I need from it: Supplementary Materials, especially Materials and Methods and figures S8–S13. The main article is already available.
   - If unavailable: Keep the report qualified and seek another full-method intervention study; confidence in this experiment's technical controls remains limited.

3. **Steven Minton, 1990, _Quantitative Results Concerning the Utility of Explanation-Based Learning_.**
   - DOI or stable URL: https://doi.org/10.1016/0004-3702(90)90059-9
   - Why I need it: Inspect the expanded generator, sampling, utility-estimation and total-cost account beyond the accessible conference paper.
   - What depends on it: How broadly retained-but-costly knowledge explains failure, and whether apparent benefit depends on omitting learning expense.
   - What I need from it: Whole paper, including experimental and utility-estimation methods.
   - If unavailable: Restrict claims to the accessible 1988 paper and the separate expensive-chunk analysis; retain the basic phenomenon but narrow its generality.
