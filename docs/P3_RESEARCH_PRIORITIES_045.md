## 1. Decision and connection to the funded objective

Retain the specified N1–N3 line for **at most one bounded acquisition-calibration comparison**, conditional on a validated complete-work budget, a complete preregistration and independent admission. Its purpose is to decide whether this particular feedback loop earns any further scientific attention. Component correctness and prior engineering effort do not justify continuing by themselves. If adequate full-work accounting or an informative comparison cannot be established, preserve this unrun design and stop this implementation branch. Do not issue another component profile simply because044 completed. [S4, S5]

This is a priority for **information gained from a decisive comparison**, not a judgment that N1–N3 is the strongest route to a field-defining advance. None of the five frozen candidates has demonstrated the funded conjunction: acquire useful knowledge through interaction with an unfamiliar world, keep it usable through a specified discontinuity, and recombine separately acquired relations for a materially new demand. The existing record supports distinctions among those requirements and weaknesses in our proposed operations. It does not yet support a calibrated probability of breakthrough for any candidate. [S1–S4]

The programme has already completed diagnosis, two separated generation passes and subsequent screening within their recorded scopes. The next step is not to invent a mechanism merely to escape an engineering problem, nor to treat a current toy as the programme's destination. The immediate scientific question remains narrow: **does N3 obtain decision-useful evidence earlier, and does N1's acquired partition contribute beyond ordinary historical conditioning?** A later substantive recombination or retention design must ask stronger questions directly. [S1, S2, S4]

## 3. The strongest confound must govern interpretation

The042 target always ends its preparation with `SET_b, MASK`. At decision time the current observation is NEUTRAL, the previous observation is CUE_b and the previous action is MASK. The final setter overwrites the earlier register value. **ORDER1 therefore already contains the short historical distinction needed by the target.** Its ordinary count table can pool the irrelevant earlier preparation histories without being supplied a semantic cue label. Longer held-out whole words do not change this fact. [S4, sections1–3]

This deduction establishes representational sufficiency, not that every finite acquired trace contains sufficient examples or that ORDER1 will always win. That remaining uncertainty is why one bounded acquisition calibration can still have diagnostic value. It may separate a useful acquisition order from a poor one. It cannot establish that N1 composes transition rules for unseen feature combinations: its absent joint keys remain uniform. [S4]

There are three leading alternatives to a favorable interpretation, already named before target results: ordinary ORDER1 conditioning; unequal counted resources or proposal overhead; and action-label tie order or count exposure. The frozen controls address these alternatives. In particular, FEEDBACK_NULL and direct COVERAGE are not two independent baseline replications: they share a schedule until an accounted resource boundary matters. Credit may contain count-fitting improvement when a new predicate opens the trial gate. Same-trace frozen readout interventions do not by themselves identify the online causal contribution of N1 to the formation of N3's credit. [S4; S6, R3–R5]

Apply the existing042 rules literally if execution is later admitted:

- Adequately observed failure of the fixed positive predicate ends the claim that this feedback package merits continuation **on this panel and allowance**. It is not a universal negative about curiosity or active learning.
- An allocation predicate that passes while the N1-specific predicate fails supports, at most, an allocation lead. If ORDER1 accounts for the readout, state that ordinary short-history conditioning suffices.
- An N1-specific predicate that also passes supports a bounded selected-partition contribution. It still does not establish retention, substantive recombination, novelty or contemporary-AI prevalence.
- Insufficient evaluability or corrupt implementation blocks scientific interpretation. Keep unavailable decisions and their bounds; do not replace them with zeros or remove them from the denominator.

No target scores were used to choose this interpretation. The original043 failed attempt and corrected044 component measurements are development evidence. A coding error is not a failed hypothesis, and correction of that error is not positive evidence for one. [S4, S5]

## 5. Returning to the broader research question

The useful outcome of this branch is a decision with a located cause. An unfavorable result can show that this specific acquisition feedback, selected partition or cost burden does not buy enough downstream usefulness. A favorable result can isolate a smaller claim worth carrying into a stronger instrument. Neither outcome warrants indefinite tuning of the042 world, budget, cue structure or thresholds.

A subsequent substantive design must require a relation acquired before its future usefulness is disclosed; an explicit discontinuity with every surviving information channel counted; and a new demand whose correct solution depends on combining that relation with separately acquired information. It must distinguish reuse from a stored complete answer, ordinary reacquisition, familiar short-key lookup and evaluator-supplied decomposition. These are requirements for an informative future comparison, not a newly specified mechanism or an admitted experiment. The current N1–N3 predictor may prove unsuitable for that demand; if so, return to specification or a separately recorded generation phase instead of enlarging its claim. [S1, S2, S4]

The archaeological and interdisciplinary work remains scientifically relevant. It supports specific distinctions—supplied versus acquired descriptions, the cost of applying retained knowledge, action-dependent access to evidence, and surviving information versus successful expression. It does not prove that old methods were uniformly abandoned or that biology validates our inventions. The existing diagnosis records both expired implementation objections and persistent information assumptions; larger hardware cannot remove missing evidence or non-identifiability. This assessment has not conducted a fresh 2026 literature sweep and makes no new historical-priority assertion. [S1, sections4–6; S3; S6]

**Further papers:** none are required for this decision from the existing evidence. The requested Chvátal and Pierce–Kuipers methods were supplied and assessed in038–041; their036 request is no longer outstanding. If a later new comparator or historical claim depends on unread methods, request that specific paper in the governing batch format. Do not demand more papers merely to defer this decision. [S6]
