# Independent review input contract: first Phase 3 audit

Prepared 2026-09-06. This file defines the review task and its evidence boundary. It is not a reviewer invocation, a verified sandbox, a preregistered treatment gate or a verdict. The current shared-tools consultations do not satisfy the independent-review requirement.

The approved claims and audit arguments are pinned by `evidence/P3_AUDIT_REVIEW_MANIFEST.json`. The manifest gives exact paths and hashes, including frozen specifications and the diagnostic observer evidence. Source-version hashes and primary-method URLs appear in the component notes. Copyrighted full papers stay outside the public repository; the reviewer may retrieve the cited legitimate full texts read-only. If an exact method version becomes unavailable, record that dependency rather than deciding from an abstract.

Before invocation, verify in the actual runtime that the reviewer receives a fresh context and enforced read-only tools, including shell commands and connector effects. Use synthetic files to demonstrate allowed reads, denied writes and any forbidden reads. A prompt that asks a fully empowered agent to behave read-only is insufficient. No mechanism treatment, paid API or unattended loop is part of this review. The human-run capability inventory is preparation for choosing that runtime; it does not establish the boundary.

## Task for the separate reviewer

Start from this packet in a fresh context, without the PI conversation. Read the approved specifications and primary methods before evaluating the reduction arguments. Treat every PI conclusion as a claim to test. Check the pinned bytes and inspect the method sections, not just the bibliography. Do not change evidence, thresholds, specifications or artifacts.

Evaluate these objects separately:

- C1's complete state/update/reset/query operation under its fixed finite model family.
- C2's experiment selector and posterior on its modeled domain, including the terminal-task disclosure assumption and explicit handling of unsupported histories.
- C3's stitching-family mapping and the distinct all-history cached-response equivalence, including actual historical information and costs.
- C4's selected-scope core, its remaining reporting/use interface, and the difference from both all-positive version-space learning and a complete AQ classifier.
- The shared proposition T: the broad principle, the incomplete approved combination, the counterexample and the explicitly completed reference comparator. Component reductions do not decide joint novelty by themselves.

For each, distinguish an exact operation reduction, a restricted family instance, a literal published implementation, an unestablished wrapper/combination claim, and an effectiveness claim. A mathematical construction that simply imports an unevaluated candidate into the comparator cannot independently settle that candidate's priority. Require the mapping of state, inputs, update, allocation, reset and cost; a separating history must escape the actual comparator rather than a weak setting of it.

The observer is a separate diagnostic result. Check whether its permitted current-state API supports the stated computation and whether any historical information is being credited to the wrong channel. This audit does not ask for a new efficacy verdict on an unrun candidate. Preserve the recorded post-run metadata-completion limitation; do not silently treat that later export as a pre-run artifact. Check the reported narrow Zheng lemma counterexample separately from the empirical recovery procedure.

Return exactly one allowed programme verdict for this audit step: `GO`, `REVISE_ONCE`, `KILL`, or `SUSPEND_FOR_DEPENDENCY`. Specify what that verdict applies to; an overall audit-step result must not silently remove or clear every component. Include separate dispositions and reasons for the objects above, exact artifact paths and SHA-256 digests, missing full methods or enforcement evidence, and any required correction. `GO` on a correct negative audit is not evidence that its reduced mechanism is novel.

The reviewer result is emitted to the human through the externally controlled output channel. The PI may prepare the packet and read the result but cannot fabricate or self-issue it. The runtime-specific invocation will be supplied after the capability report, using existing authorized resources only.

## Before requesting the verdict

The ledger must record the strongest three objections and the check of the strongest. For this analytical audit, the relevant checks are substitution/induction and explicit counterexamples, not a fabricated treatment score. No new candidate measurement is required merely to validate the written algebra. If a computational treatment is proposed to resolve an additional scientific claim, first freeze its numerical thresholds and verifier under the programme's full controls.
